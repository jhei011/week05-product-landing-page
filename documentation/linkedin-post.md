🚀 Week 5 – Client-Server Technologies

This week I designed and built a responsive Product Landing Page for Ember & Oak Coffee Co.,
a neighborhood coffee shop, using Laravel, Tailwind CSS, and Blade Components. Rather than a
typical tech mockup, I based it on a real small business — reimagining a local coffee shop's
ordering and subscription experience as a modern, mobile-friendly landing page.

Along the way I focused on:
☕ Component-based development — 9 reusable Blade Components (navbar, hero, feature cards,
   pricing cards, testimonial cards, buttons, footer, and more) instead of repeated HTML
🎨 A custom Tailwind design system — a warm espresso/amber/cream palette and Fraunces + Inter
   typography pairing, so every section feels like one cohesive brand
📱 Full responsive design — tested and refined across desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints

Comparing my initial wireframe with the final polished interface really showed how much
thoughtful spacing, color, and component structure can improve both the look and the
maintainability of a UI.
